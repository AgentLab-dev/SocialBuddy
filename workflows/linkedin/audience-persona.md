# Workflow: audience and persona

## Purpose

Name **who the operator is writing for** and what those people must believe or do. Personas are hypotheses, not surveillance files.

## Inputs

- Operator goals (hire, be hired, attract buyers, peer reputation).
- Eligible public facts (what credibility is real).
- Optional: anonymized CRM *segments* (not named contacts) if sensitivity allows.

## Procedure

1. Write 1–3 personas as **jobs-to-be-done**, not demographics stereotypes.
   - Example shape: “Analytics eng lead evaluating whether to introduce contracts.”
2. For each persona: prior knowledge, forbidden jargon, proof they accept (code, numbers, war stories), channels they actually read.
3. Map pillars from content planning to personas. If a pillar maps to nobody, cut it.
4. **Do not** scrape “people also viewed” or build shadow profiles. Official ads/analytics products, if added later, are a different adapter.
5. Output: persona cards labeled `hypothesis`, plus 3 conversation questions each.

## Safety

- No real candidate/lead names in persona docs that might be committed.
- Do not infer race, health, or politics.
- Personas do not justify spam.
